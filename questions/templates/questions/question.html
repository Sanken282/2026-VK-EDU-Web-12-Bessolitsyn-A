How to build a moon park?

Building a moon park requires careful planning of life support systems, radiation shielding, and sustainable energy sources to withstand extreme temperature fluctuations from -173°C to 127°C. Construction materials would likely include lunar regolith processed into building blocks to reduce transportation costs from Earth. Additionally, the park needs reliable communication systems, emergency protocols, and recreational facilities designed for low-gravity environments.
